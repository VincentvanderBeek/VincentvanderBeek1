# 🏹 Bow-Only Build — Elden Ring + Shadow of the Erdtree

A ranged-only run: no melee weapons used for attacking. Two finished setups below — one you can complete in the base game, one for after the DLC — followed by the swaps, the stats, and the checklist of bows actually worth carrying.

---

# ⭐ The setups

## Base game end setup

| Slot | Item | Role |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Main bow** | **Composite Bow** + Rain of Arrows | Light-bow moveset: fire out of rolls, jumps, sprints and from Torrent. Infusible, so the ash is your choice. Weighs almost nothing. |
| **Mobile medium** | **Black Bow** | Same roll-and-jump moveset at medium-bow range and damage. Locked to Barrage and cannot be infused — that is its one real flaw. |
| **Sniper** | **Golem Greatbow** + Golem's Great Arrows | Highest raw attack rating of any bow in the game. Locked to Through and Through, which pierces straight through a line of enemies. |
| **Boss burst** | **Lion Greatbow** + Radahn's Spears | Radahn's Rain fires about 50% more projectiles than Rain of Arrows. Radahn's Spears add a 20% damage buff on top. |
| **Status** | **Serpent Bow** | Niche pick: +15 poison on every arrow, Arcane scaling speeds status build-up. Swapped in for long fights only. |

**Talismans:** Arrow's Sting · Arrow's Reach · Shard of Alexander · Ritual Sword
**Spells:** Howl of Shabriri + Golden Vow (or Flame, Grant Me Strength + Golden Vow at low Faith — Shabriri and Flame do not stack)
**Physick:** Stonebarb Cracked Tear + Opaline Hardtear
**Ammo:** Bone Great Arrow (Fletched) in bulk · Golem's Great Arrows for the sniper · Radahn's Spears for the Lion · status arrows on the Composite

## DLC end setup

| Slot | Item | Role |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Main bow** | **Ansbach's Longbow** + Fan Shot | The defining bow of the DLC. Light-bow moveset with medium-bow damage, and Fan Shot throws eight arrows for 11 FP. Needs **43 DEX**. |
| **Boss burst** | **Lion Greatbow** + Radahn's Spears | Unchanged. Still the best greatbow for melting large bosses, especially in corridors. |
| **Sniper** | **Golem Greatbow**, or **Greatbow** with Igon's Drake Hunt | Golem for raw power; the infusible Greatbow pulls ahead at high levels thanks to quality scaling. |
| **Status** | **Bone Bow** + Stormwing Bone Arrows | Rancor Shot costs 3 FP and tracks hard. A post-DLC patch gutted status build-up in PvE, so this is for damage and tracking now, not procs. |

**Talismans:** Arrow's Sting · Arrow's Soaring Sting · Shard of Alexander · Ritual Sword
**Armor:** Rakshasa's Set — 2% extra damage per piece
**Everything else:** same spells, same physick, same ammo plan

The DLC setup costs **no extra stat points** if you follow the spread below: 55 Dexterity already covers Ansbach's 43.

## Talisman swaps

Two slots are locked, two are yours. **Locked:** Arrow's Sting always, plus Arrow's Reach — or Arrow's Soaring Sting with the DLC, since those two cannot be worn together. **Flex:** the Shard of Alexander and Ritual Sword slots.

| Swap in | Takes the slot of | When |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Dragoncrest Greatshield Talisman | Ritual Sword | Shabriri's +25% damage taken is what keeps killing you. −20% physical is the biggest survival swing available. |
| Winged Sword Insignia | Shard of Alexander | You fire plain shots more than ashes of war. Rotten Winged Sword Insignia is the straight upgrade once you reach the Haligtree. |
| Sharpshot Talisman *(DLC)* | Ritual Sword | You fight locked on and aimed rather than firing into crowds. |
| Enraged Divine Beast Talisman *(DLC)* | Ritual Sword | Running Stormwing Bone Arrows on the Bone Bow or Ansbach's. |
| Lord of Blood's Exultation | Ritual Sword | Bleed arrows, and the blood loss actually triggers. |
| Godfrey Icon | Shard of Alexander | Mighty Shot is your main skill. It boosts charged skills only — nothing for Rain of Arrows or Radahn's Rain. |
| Radagon's Soreseal | Ritual Sword | Mid-run, before you have the levels. Drop it once Howl of Shabriri is online — the two damage-taken penalties stack badly. |

# 📊 Stats

## Where to end up

Every stat here is a range. The minimum is what the gear and spells actually demand; the maximum is the soft cap where points stop paying well. Anywhere between the two is a legitimate build.

| Stat | Min | Max | What the range means |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Vigor | 40 | 60 | 40 and 60 are the two soft caps. 40 is survivable at range; 60 is the answer if Shabriri's +25% damage taken keeps killing you. |
| Mind | 20 | 30 | Plain shots cost nothing. 20 covers occasional skills, 30 covers Radahn's Rain and Fan Shot spam. |
| Endurance | 25 | 40 | 25 is the equip-load soft cap and enough for one greatbow plus light armour. 40 lets you carry two greatbows and still medium-roll. |
| Strength | 24 | 55 | 24 lifts the Golem Greatbow. 55 is the soft cap and real damage, since every greatbow scales D in Strength. |
| Dexterity | 20 | 55 | 20 wields everything in the base game. **43** unlocks Ansbach's Longbow. 55 is the soft cap and your main scaling stat. |
| Intelligence | 9 | 17 | Unused unless you want the Golden Order Seal at 17. Buffs cast identically from any seal, so this is optional. |
| Faith | 15 | 33 | 15 for Flame, Grant Me Strength. 25 for Golden Vow. **33 for Howl of Shabriri**, the most expensive requirement in the build. |
| Arcane | 11 | 30 | 11 wields the Serpent Bow. 30 only if poison and rot are genuinely the plan — Arcane boosts status build-up, not damage. |

Minimums throughout land near **level 100**. Maximums throughout land near **level 170**. A sensible finish sits in the middle, around 140.

## The floors

| Item | Requires |
| --- | --- |
| Golem Greatbow | 24 STR / 18 DEX — **highest Strength in the kit** |
| Ansbach's Longbow | 9 STR / **43 DEX** — **highest Dexterity in the kit** |
| Lion Greatbow | 22 STR / 18 DEX |
| Greatbow | 20 STR / 20 DEX |
| Black Bow | 9 STR / 20 DEX |
| Composite Bow | 15 STR / 15 DEX |
| Serpent Bow | 8 STR / 15 DEX / 11 ARC |
| Howl of Shabriri | **33 FAI** — **highest Faith in the kit** |
| Golden Vow (incantation) | 25 FAI, or **0** as an ash of war on a dagger |
| Flame, Grant Me Strength | 15 FAI |
| Finger Seal | 10 FAI |

**Absolute floor: 24 STR, 20 DEX, 15 FAI, 11 ARC.** Add 43 DEX for Ansbach's and 33 FAI for Shabriri once you want them.

## Priority order

1. **Clear the floors.** Nothing works until these are in.
2. **Dexterity to 55.** Scales every bow you carry and passes Ansbach's 43 on the way.
3. **Endurance to 25, then 30+.** Two greatbows plus armour is what decides your roll.
4. **Strength to 55.** Pays on every greatbow once Dexterity is capped.
5. **Vigor from 40 toward 60**, sooner if you are running Shabriri.
6. **To taste:** Mind to 30 if you run dry mid-boss, Arcane to 30 if you commit to status.

Buff incantations have fixed effects and do not scale with a seal, so any seal casts them identically. Use the cheapest one you can wield — the Finger Seal at 10 Faith does the same job as the Golden Order Seal at 17/17.

# ✨ Buffs

| Buff | Requires | Effect | Where |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| [Howl of Shabriri](https://eldenring.wiki.fextralife.com/Howl+of+Shabriri) | **33 FAI**, 21 FP | +30% attack, +25% damage taken | Chest on the second floor of the Frenzy-Flaming Tower, Liurnia |
| [Golden Vow](https://eldenring.wiki.fextralife.com/Golden+Vow) | 25 FAI, or 0 as an ash | +15% attack, +10% defence | Corpse Stench Shack, Mt. Gelmir (ash: corpse by the lava) |
| [Flame, Grant Me Strength](https://eldenring.wiki.fextralife.com/Flame,+Grant+Me+Strength) | 15 FAI | +20% physical and fire attack | Fort Gael, Caelid |
| [Exalted Flesh](https://eldenring.wiki.fextralife.com/Exalted+Flesh) | — | +20% physical attack | Armorer's Cookbook (3) |
| [Pickled Turtle Neck](https://eldenring.wiki.fextralife.com/Pickled+Turtle+Neck) | — | Faster stamina recovery | Nomadic Warrior's Cookbook (3) |

**Howl of Shabriri and Flame, Grant Me Strength do not stack** — they occupy the same buff category and overwrite each other. Golden Vow stacks with either. So the two real options are:

- **Budget (15 FAI):** Flame, Grant Me Strength + Golden Vow as an ash of war on a cheap dagger. No Faith needed for the Vow at all.
- **Full (33 FAI):** Howl of Shabriri + Golden Vow. Stronger, but you pay 18 extra Faith levels and take 25% more damage.

Bows **cannot be weapon-buffed** — no greases, no Holy Weapon, nothing. Only body buffs that raise your own attack rating carry over to arrows. That is why this list is short.

**Physick:** [Stonebarb Cracked Tear](https://eldenring.wiki.fextralife.com/Stonebarb+Cracked+Tear) boosts stance-breaking attacks, which is exactly what greatbows do. Pair it with [Opaline Hardtear](https://eldenring.wiki.fextralife.com/Opaline+Hardtear) to offset Shabriri, or [Cerulean Hidden Tear](https://eldenring.wiki.fextralife.com/Cerulean+Hidden+Tear) when you want to spam skills through a boss opener.

# 📌 Notes that shape the run

- **No weapon buffs on bows.** Damage comes from the bow, the arrow, talismans and body buffs only.
- **Moveset beats stats.** The Composite Bow, Black Bow and Ansbach's Longbow fire out of rolls, jumps and sprints. Greatbows cannot fire from Torrent or while moving at all. That gap matters more than any scaling difference on these tables.
- **Arrow choice is a damage stat.** The right arrows on a plain bow beat the wrong arrows on a great one.
- **Scadutree Blessing outranks your level in the DLC.** It scales all damage dealt and taken in the shadow realm. A level 150 character at Blessing 3 gets flattened by things a level 120 at Blessing 12 walks through — collect fragments before you worry about respeccing.
