## Rules that shape a bow-only run

That is all 19 bows — 6 light bows, 8 bows, 5 greatbows. A few mechanics decide what is actually usable:

- No bow of any class can be buffed with greases, spells or consumable weapon buffs. Your damage comes from the bow, the arrow, talismans and body buffs only.
- Light bows and the Black Bow / Ansbach's Longbow can fire out of a roll, sprint, jump or landing. Everything else cannot, and that gap is bigger than any stat difference on these tables.
- Light bows and the plain Greatbow take ashes of war. Most unique bows do not.
- Arrow choice is a damage stat. A gimmick bow with the wrong arrows is worse than a plain one with the right arrows.
- Ammunition is the real bottleneck. Buy bell bearings, unlock crafting cookbooks early, and carry two arrow types minimum.

| Greatbow | 20 STR / 20 DEX — **highest DEX requirement in the kit** |

| Serpent Bow | 8 STR / 15 DEX / **11 ARC** |
| Howl of Shabriri | 21 FAI |

| [Finger Seal](https://eldenring.wiki.fextralife.com/Finger+Seal) | 10 FAI |
| [Golden Order Seal](https://eldenring.wiki.fextralife.com/Golden+Order+Seal) | 17 FAI / 17 INT |

So the floor is **22 STR, 20 DEX, 21 FAI, 17 INT, 11 ARC**. Everything past that is damage, health or carry weight.

On the Golden Order Seal: buff incantations have fixed effects and do not scale with a seal's incantation scaling, so any seal casts both your buffs at exactly the same strength. You are carrying this one because it turns up early rather than because it is stronger, and the 17 INT is the toll for that. If you would rather keep those points, the Gravel Stone Seal does the identical job at 10 FAI.

### What to end on

Only the floors are fixed. Past those, most of these are a range rather than a number — pick the end of the range that matches how you actually play.

| Stat | End on | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Vigor | 40, or 60 | 40 is the first soft cap and holds up fine at range. Go 60 if you would rather have the cushion — Howl of Shabriri adds 25% damage taken, so the hits that do land hurt more. |
| Mind | 30 | Your buffs and your ashes of war draw on the same pool, and one boss opener can empty it. |
| Endurance | 30 | Equip load soft caps at 25, and both greatbows together are 19.5 of load before armour. This stat decides your roll. |
| Strength | 22, or 55 | 22 lifts the Lion Greatbow, which is all you need if the Black Bow does most of your shooting. Both greatbows scale D in Strength, so go 55 when the Lion is carrying your damage. |
| Dexterity | **55** | Soft cap, and the scaling stat on all five bows. This is where damage comes from. |
| Intelligence | 17 | 17 for the Golden Order Seal. It buys access, not power — see the note above. |
| Faith | 21 | Set exactly by Howl of Shabriri. Flame, Grant Me Strength only needs 15. |
| Arcane | 11, or 30 | 11 wields the Serpent Bow. Status build-up scales with Arcane, so 30 is worth it if poison and rot are the plan. |

### Priority order

1. **Clear the floors:** 22 STR, 20 DEX, 21 FAI, 11 ARC, and 17 INT if you want the Golden Order Seal. Nothing works until these are in.
2. **Dexterity to 55.** The Black Bow's scaling stat, and it scales the greatbows too. This is your main damage now.
3. **Strength to 55** only if the Lion Greatbow is doing the killing. Otherwise stop at 22 and spend elsewhere.
4. **Endurance to 30.** The Lion and the Greatbow are 9.5 and 10.0, and that is what decides your roll.
5. **Vigor to 40**, or 60 if Shabriri keeps getting you killed.
6. **Then to taste:** Arcane to 30 if you keep the Serpent Bow, Mind to 30 if you run dry mid-boss.

### Worth a Larval Tear

If Faith has crept above 21, a respec at Rennala turns the excess straight into Strength or Dexterity at no cost. Faith past the Shabriri requirement does nothing for you — buff incantations have fixed effects and no scaling.

## Honourable mentions from the DLC

Everything above is base game. If you bring Shadow of the Erdtree into the run, four things change the picture:

- **[Ansbach's Longbow](https://eldenring.wiki.fextralife.com/Ansbach's+Longbow)** — the Black Bow's moveset with better damage. Costs 43 DEX, which is a build decision, not a pickup.
- **[Igon's Greatbow](https://eldenring.wiki.fextralife.com/Igon's+Greatbow)** — outdamages both greatbows on single targets and shreds dragons with Igon's Harpoon.
- **[Arrow's Soaring Sting Talisman](https://eldenring.wiki.fextralife.com/Arrow's+Soaring+Sting+Talisman)** — +8% damage and +50% range. It cannot be worn with Arrow's Reach, but it stacks with Arrow's Sting for about +18.8% arrow damage across two slots. This is the single biggest upgrade to the loadout above.
- **[Sharpshot Talisman](https://eldenring.wiki.fextralife.com/Sharpshot+Talisman)** — boosts locked-on precision shots. Worth a slot only if you aim rather than fire-and-forget.

[Bone Bow](https://eldenring.wiki.fextralife.com/Bone+Bow) and the [Outer God Heirloom](https://eldenring.wiki.fextralife.com/Outer+God+Heirloom) are listed above for completeness, but neither displaces anything in this setup.
## Optimised builds

Two finished versions of the same build. The base-game one is complete on its own; the DLC one is the same build with three swaps.

### Base game only

- **Main:** Black Bow. Best bow in the game and the only base-game one on the light-bow moveset, so you keep firing while moving.
- **Damage:** Lion Greatbow with Radahn's Rain, loaded with Golem's Great Arrows or Radahn's Spears.
- **Flexible heavy:** Greatbow with Through and Through for single targets, Rain of Arrows for status and chip.
- **Backup:** Composite Bow with Barrage, for anything that closes to melee range.
- **Utility:** Serpent Bow for poison, swapped in only when a fight runs long enough for status to land.
- **Talismans:** Arrow's Sting, Arrow's Reach, Shard of Alexander, Ritual Sword Talisman — see the swap table above for the two flex slots.
- **Spells:** Flame, Grant Me Strength and Howl of Shabriri, cast off any seal.
- **Physick:** Stonebarb Cracked Tear plus Opaline Hardtear, which cancels most of the Shabriri penalty. Swap Opaline for Cerulean Hidden Tear when you want to spam skills instead.
- **Ammo:** Bone Great Arrow (Fletched) in bulk from Nomadic Warrior's 13, Golden Great Arrows and Radahn's Spears for bosses, Golem's Great Arrows farmed from Golem Archers.
- **Stats at the finish:** 40 VIG / 25 MIND / 30 END / 30 STR / 55 DEX / 17 INT / 21 FAI / 11 ARC. That lands around level 150.

### With Shadow of the Erdtree

Only three DLC items actually beat their base-game counterparts, and none of them costs a stat point — 55 Dexterity already covers Ansbach's 43, and 30 Strength already covers Igon's 23.

- **Ansbach's Longbow replaces the Black Bow.** Same moveset, better damage.
- **Igon's Greatbow replaces the Lion Greatbow.** More raw single-target damage, plus Igon's Drake Hunt. The trade is real though: you give up Radahn's Rain, the best crowd skill on any bow. If you fight groups often, carry both — you have the Endurance for it at 30.
- **Arrow's Soaring Sting replaces Arrow's Reach.** It cannot be worn with Arrow's Reach, but paired with Arrow's Sting it stacks to roughly +18.8% arrow damage.

What did not make the cut: the **Sharpshot Talisman** only helps locked-on aimed shots, so it loses to Ritual Sword the moment you fire Radahn's Rain into a crowd. The **Bone Bow** is worse than the Composite. The **Outer God Heirloom** does nothing at 11 Arcane.

The real DLC stat is **Scadutree Blessing**, not anything on your level screen. Blessing level scales all damage you deal and take in the shadow realm, so a level 150 character at Blessing 3 will be flattened by things a level 120 at Blessing 12 walks through. At Blessing 0 the DLC gear will not save you; at Blessing 12 the base-game kit is already fine.
