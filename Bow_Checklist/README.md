## Rules that shape a bow-only run

That is all 19 bows — 6 light bows, 8 bows, 5 greatbows. A few mechanics decide what is actually usable:

- No bow of any class can be buffed with greases, spells or consumable weapon buffs. Your damage comes from the bow, the arrow, talismans and body buffs only.
- Light bows and the Black Bow / Ansbach's Longbow can fire out of a roll, sprint, jump or landing. Everything else cannot, and that gap is bigger than any stat difference on these tables.
- Light bows and the plain Greatbow take ashes of war. Most unique bows do not.
- Arrow choice is a damage stat. A gimmick bow with the wrong arrows is worse than a plain one with the right arrows.
- Ammunition is the real bottleneck. Buy bell bearings, unlock crafting cookbooks early, and carry two arrow types minimum.

## Seals and buff tools

One thing to fix in your plan: bows cannot be weapon-buffed. Only **body buffs** — the ones that raise your own attack rating — carry over to arrows. So the Golden Epitaph's Last Rites is dead weight on a bow run, while the Golden Vow ash on a cheap dagger is not.

| Item | Req | Why carry it | Where |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| [Finger Seal](https://eldenring.wiki.fextralife.com/Finger+Seal) | 10 FAI | Cheapest way to cast body buffs | Twin Maiden Husks, 800 runes |
| [Golden Order Seal](https://eldenring.wiki.fextralife.com/Golden+Order+Seal) | 17 FAI / 17 INT | Only if you are already INT/FAI | Minor Erdtree Church, Altus Plateau |
| [Ash of War: Golden Vow](https://eldenring.wiki.fextralife.com/Ash_of_War:_Golden_Vow) on a dagger | 5 STR / 9 DEX | +15% attack, +10% defence, no FAI needed | Corpse by the lava, Mt. Gelmir |

The dagger-plus-Golden-Vow trick is the one every bow build should carry: a Misericorde or any light dagger in the off-hand, swapped in, skill used, swapped back. It costs one weapon slot and no Faith investment.

## Honourable mentions from the DLC

Everything above is base game. If you bring Shadow of the Erdtree into the run, four things change the picture:

- **[Ansbach's Longbow](https://eldenring.wiki.fextralife.com/Ansbach's+Longbow)** — the Black Bow's moveset with better damage. Costs 43 DEX, which is a build decision, not a pickup.
- **[Igon's Greatbow](https://eldenring.wiki.fextralife.com/Igon's+Greatbow)** — outdamages both greatbows on single targets and shreds dragons with Igon's Harpoon.
- **[Arrow's Soaring Sting Talisman](https://eldenring.wiki.fextralife.com/Arrow's+Soaring+Sting+Talisman)** — +8% damage and +50% range. It cannot be worn with Arrow's Reach, but it stacks with Arrow's Sting for about +18.8% arrow damage across two slots. This is the single biggest upgrade to the loadout above.
- **[Sharpshot Talisman](https://eldenring.wiki.fextralife.com/Sharpshot+Talisman)** — boosts locked-on precision shots. Worth a slot only if you aim rather than fire-and-forget.

[Bone Bow](https://eldenring.wiki.fextralife.com/Bone+Bow) and the [Outer God Heirloom](https://eldenring.wiki.fextralife.com/Outer+God+Heirloom) are listed above for completeness, but neither displaces anything in this setup.
